Plenty of space, and a corner for whatever you’re doing.

The Arrosto is a large, modern restaurant with comfortable seating and warm lighting. The floor is laid out in separate corners, so a quiet table for two and a long table of friends can sit in the same room without getting in each other’s way.

Reviewers describe it as calm and quiet, a good fit for friends, family or a date. There is also a private dining room and rooftop seating.
